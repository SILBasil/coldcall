# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Managers and administrators/agents who coordinate, assign, track, and make outbound calls to leads and retention customers (ลูกค้าประจำ).

## Product Purpose
A premium, responsive CRM tailored for cold calling and lead lifecycle management (from raw pool to qualified leads to purchasing customers), ensuring high agent productivity and clear dashboard statistics.

## Brand Personality
- **Professional & Premium**: Clean layout using a refined Sky/Slate palette.
- **Trustworthy**: Secure, clear data display with solid visual hierarchy.
- **Responsive & Alive**: Smooth micro-interactions and active hover states.

## Anti-references
- Outdated grey-heavy flat tables with zero rhythm.
- Over-nested cards and heavy drop-shadows.
- Low-contrast light grey text that is difficult to read.
- Generic SaaS "purple gradient" elements.

## Design Principles
1. **Flawless Readability**: Keep Thai typography (Anuphan font paired with Inter) readable, with proper contrast ratios and line spacing.
2. **Action-Oriented Flow**: Layout views with clear, prioritized buttons (verb + object labels) and distinct states (empty, loading, error).
3. **Harmonious Rhythm**: Use consistent spacing tokens and rounded corners (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-3xl`) for a premium modern feel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratios at least 4.5:1 for body copy.
- Full responsiveness supporting desktop, tablet, and mobile views.
- Keyboard navigable controls and screen-reader friendly descriptive links.
